Profile

Eng. Hani Al Sahooly has spent more than two decades as Projects Director, Project Manager and Senior Architect — leading the design and construction-supervision teams behind hospitals, healthcare and medical facilities.

His work spans architecture, urban planning, landscape design and project management: design, design review and redesign through to supervision of works and renovation. It also covers landscaping, administration, bills of quantities, contract documents, specifications and cost estimation.

As project manager he is responsible for planning, coordination and follow-up of design implementation, programming and scheduling, work distribution, quality control, tender preparation and agreements, and financial and cost control.
