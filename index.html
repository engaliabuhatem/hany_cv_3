About

Architect, planner and project leader for the facilities communities depend on.

Eng. Hani Al Sahooly is a Project Manager and Senior Architect at HS Group in Abu Dhabi, with 20 years at the firm. Across more than two decades as Projects Director, Project Manager and Senior Architect, he has led the design and construction-supervision teams behind many hospitals, healthcare and medical facilities.

His work spans architecture, urban planning, landscape design and project management — from design, design review and redesign through to supervision of works and renovation. It also covers landscaping, administration, bills of quantities, contract documents, specifications and cost estimation.

As a project manager he is responsible for planning, coordination and follow-up of design implementation, programming and scheduling, work distribution, quality control, tender preparation and agreements, and financial and cost control.
