01Profile

Eng. Hani Al Sahooly has spent more than two decades as Projects Director, Project Manager and Senior Architect, leading the design and construction-supervision teams behind many hospitals, healthcare and medical facilities.

He works across architecture, urban planning, landscape design and project management: design, design review and redesign through to supervision of works and renovation. His remit also covers landscaping, administration, bills of quantities, contract documents, specifications and cost estimation.

As project manager he is responsible for planning, coordination and follow-up of design implementation, programming and scheduling, work distribution, quality control, tender preparation and agreements, and financial and cost control. He has been with HS Group for 20 years.
